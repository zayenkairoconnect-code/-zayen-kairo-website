My work centres on practical technology topics, from programming and operating systems to computer networking, cybersecurity and emerging artificial intelligence tools.

I believe technology should be understandable to everyone willing to learn. That's why my approach focuses on breaking complicated concepts into simpler explanations, practical examples and useful learning experiences.

Through my books, I aim to help readers move beyond simply understanding concepts and towards applying their knowledge in the real world.

This website brings together my published books, companion learning resources and ideas for curious readers.
